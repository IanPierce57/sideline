CREDITS (the fine print)

Everyone on Sideline is fictional. Hal Brennan, Royce Stanhope, Deacon Pettiford, Lonnie Fairweather, and every caller are made-up characters. Everything they say is AI-generated opinion, written by Claude. Callers are never real people.

Not affiliated. Sideline isn't connected to or endorsed by the NBA, the NFL, ESPN, or any team, player, or broadcaster. Team and player names are used only to talk about them.

Stats. Every number on the show is pulled from one source and checked against a second; anything that can't be confirmed is cut. Data via nflverse (CC BY 4.0) · NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse · FTN Data via nflverse (CC BY-SA 4.0) · Sleeper · muffed.ai · Wikipedia and Wikidata (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Scoring. A panel of three AI judges scores every argument without knowing who made it, on evidence, reasoning, rebuttal, impact, and clarity. Hal announces their result; he doesn't pick the winner.

Headlines. The ticker shows real headlines exactly as the outlets wrote them, via GNews (free plan, so they run a few hours behind). A story appears only if at least two outlets report it, and they're named after it.
